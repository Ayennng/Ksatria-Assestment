Privacy Policy & Terms

Kasatria People Table is a technical assessment project for a job application. It is not a commercial service.

What we access

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with this page. They are used only to show who is signed in.

Where it is stored

Your profile is kept in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you sign out or close the tab. Nothing is sent to or stored on any server run by this project.

Sharing

Your information is not sold, shared or used for any other purpose.

Terms of use

The page is provided as-is, for demonstration only. The people data shown comes from the sample dataset supplied for the assessment.
